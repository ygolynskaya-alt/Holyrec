08 — FAQ

Good to know

Anything else, just ask on Instagram or by email.

How do I book?

Send a message with your idea and dates. We start with a free 20-minute call, then you get a plan and a quote. A 50% deposit holds your date.

When do I get my photos and videos?

A first selection within a few days, final retouched images and edits within 1–2 weeks, depending on the project.

I'm camera shy. Will this work for me?

Yes. Years of directing live TV taught me how to put people at ease. I guide every pose and line, so you never have to guess.

Do you travel?

Based in Calgary and available across Alberta and on location. Travel outside the city is quoted separately.

Can I book strategy only, or social media only?

Yes. Book one service or the full cycle, from the plan to the posting.
